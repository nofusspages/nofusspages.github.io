# NoFussPages Pin Scheduler – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 25, 2026*

NoFussPages Pin Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owner of the NoFussPages Pinterest account to publish pins of NoFussPages' own products to that account.

**What it stores.** Only the access token Pinterest provides, kept on the owner's private computer. It is used solely to create pins and boards on the owner's own Pinterest account.

**What it does not do.** It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other Pinterest users, and it does not read anyone else's pins or boards.

**Your control.** The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in Pinterest settings (Settings > Security and logins > Apps).
